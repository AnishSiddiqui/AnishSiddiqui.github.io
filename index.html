3D-Printed Autonomous Rover Model

Lunar Terrain Vehicle Prototype

Designed and constructed a fully functional 3D-printed autonomous rover inspired by NASA’s Lunar Terrain Vehicle (LTV) program. The outer structure was originally modeled in FreeCad before transitioning to Onshape to integrate electrical components. The rover features dual ESP32 microcontrollers, rear DC gear motors, and a front Ackermann steering system, with a customized chassis fabricated on a Creality Ender-3 S1. It supports wireless control via a PS4 controller, live video streaming through an ESP32-CAM, and autonomous navigation using an MPU6050 IMU and a rotating TF-Luna LiDAR mounted on a servo for environmental mapping. The system also includes custom power regulation and a modular 3D-printed platform to accommodate and support all onboard components.

Customized H-bridge motor driver

Engineered and built a custom H-bridge motor drive from distinct electrical components to regulate the direction and speed of a DC motor. The circuit uses a four-transistor configuration arranged in an H-bridge topology, allowing bidirectional current flow through the motor by selectively activating diagonal transistor pairs. Incorporated proper gate control using resistors and logic-level MOSFETs, along with flyback protection and decoupling capacitors to ensure stable and safe operation. The driver was interfaced with a microcontroller to enable forward, reverse, braking, and PWM-based speed control.

Industry-recognized standard for electronic assemblies, focused on teaching proper assembly, soldering, inspection, and quality control of PCB components.
